## What this page covers

This page explains what the site itself collects when you read it. What is collected by reactions, comments or analytics, who handles it, and what choices you have are described in the section at the end of this page, for the features the site has switched on.

## Server logs

The site is delivered by a hosting provider. Like almost every web host, it keeps ordinary request logs: your IP address, the time of the request, the page you asked for and basic details about your browser. These logs are used to run and protect the service and are kept for the provider's own retention period. The author does not use them to identify individual readers.

## Browser storage

The site stores a few small settings in your browser: your colour mode (light, dark or system) and any posts you have saved to read later. This information stays on your device. The site itself keeps no reader accounts and does not track you across other websites.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. To use these rights, or with any privacy question, contact the author using the contact details on the site.
